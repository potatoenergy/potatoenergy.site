### Diun: Guardian of the relevance of containers [🔄](https://github.com/crazy-max/diun)

**Personal assistant** for updating Docker images.

**What does:**

- 🔍 Monitors image tags in Docker Hub, GitHub, and private registries
- 📩 Sends notifications to Telegram, Discord when new versions are released
- 📋 Detailed log of changes with links to releases

**How to use:**

1. Diun works in the background - no configuration required
2. You will receive a notification when an update is available for the service.
3. The administrator applies the update or sets up an auto-update

**For administrators:**
Flexible rules, filtering, and notifications are all in the config.
