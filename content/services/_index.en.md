### Technology at Work

The **Potato Energy services ecosystem** is designed to make complex technologies work seamlessly, providing simple access to digital capabilities. All services are integrated with each other, ensuring a single point of entry.

**Key Areas**

- 🛡️ **Security**: single sign-on through Authelia with two-factor authentication and flexible access rights management
- 🖥️ **Infrastructure**: routing through Traefik and monitoring through Grafana for stable operation of all services
- ☁️ **Storage**: personal Nextcloud cloud with collaborative document editing and video conferencing support
- 🏠 **Smart Home**: automation with Home Assistant, connecting devices into one system without cloud data sharing
- 🎮 **Gaming**: optimized Minecraft and Mindustry servers with Discord integration

**Advantages**

- ✅ All services work as a unified whole through a single authentication system
- ✅ Open standards and compatibility with other platforms
- ✅ Privacy and security of data - information is not transferred to third parties
- ✅ Unified design even in emergency situations
- ✅ Regular updates and monitoring to prevent issues
