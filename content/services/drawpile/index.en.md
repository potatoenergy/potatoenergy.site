### Drawpile: Collaborative Drawing [🎨](https://drawpile.net/)

**Cooperative server** for real-time drawing.

**Functions:**

- ✏️ Drawing together on the same canvas: up to 8+ users
- 🖌️ Tools: layers, brushes, selection, transformation
- 💬 Built-in chat for coordination
- 🎥 Recording sessions with playback
- 📤 Exporting the result to PNG/PSD

**How to connect:**

1. Download the client from [drawpile.net](https://drawpile.net/download) or launch the [online version](https://web.drawpile.net/) in the browser
2. Run → "Connect" → enter the address: `connect.potatoenergy.ru:27750`
3. Choose a nickname, join the session, or create your own

**Rules:**
Respect the creativity of others, don't spam in the chat, coordinate actions. Details can be found in the session description.
