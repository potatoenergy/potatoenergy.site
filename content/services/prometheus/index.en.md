### Prometheus: Infrastructure Monitoring [📈](https://prometheus.io/)

**Warning system** for potential issues before they occur.  
**Monitors:**

- Availability of all services
- Server load
- Abnormal activity
- Service level agreements  
**How to use:** Runs in the background, managed by administrators.
